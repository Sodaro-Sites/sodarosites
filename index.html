Straight answers.

Is the demo really free?

Yes. We build a working preview of your new site at no cost and with no obligation. If you don't love it, you owe nothing.

When do I pay?

Only after you've seen and approved your demo. You pay the full package price once, through a secure Stripe checkout. There's no deposit and no surprise invoice later.

Who owns the domain?

You do. You buy your domain in your own name (usually about $10–20 a year from any registrar), and we point your new site at it.

Are there monthly fees?

Not required. Hosting runs on GitHub Pages, which is free. Sodaro Care ($49 or $99/month) is optional, for owners who want updates, backups and monitoring handled for them.

What if I ever want to leave?

You get your site's code. Your domain was always yours. No hostage situations.

Do you only work with local businesses?

No. We build for any business, anywhere. Local Hero is designed around local businesses; Better Than Corporate fits bigger or multi-location operations.
