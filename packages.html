Payment, ownership, revisions

The fine print, in plain English.

When exactly do I pay?

After you've seen your free demo and told us you love it. Then you pay the full package price once through the secure Stripe link above. There's no deposit and nothing to pay before your demo.

What if I don't like the demo?

Then you don't pay anything. Tell us what you'd change and we can adjust it, or walk away. No hard feelings, no bill.

Is checkout secure?

Yes. Payments are processed by Stripe; we never see or store your card number. Your receipt will come from Sodaro Sites.

Who owns my domain and my site?

You buy your domain in your own name, so it's always yours. We host the finished site and keep it running. If you ever leave, you get your site's code.

What counts as a revision round?

One round is a single list of changes you send us at once (wording, photos, colors, layout tweaks), which we apply together. Local Hero includes 2 rounds, Better Than Corporate includes 3.

What happens after launch?

Local Hero includes 14 days of fix support; Better Than Corporate includes 30 days of priority changes. After that, Sodaro Care is optional, or you can just text us when you need something and we'll quote it.

How fast is "live"?

Local Hero goes live within 7 days of payment, Better Than Corporate within 14. The clock depends on getting your domain access and any photos or details we still need from you.
